Brainpath Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

The short version. Brainpath has no accounts and no login. Your progress is stored on your device. We collect anonymous usage events tied to a random install ID so we can see how the app is used. Google AdMob shows ads and collects device identifiers to do so. If you buy Brainpath Plus or an extra test, payment happens on our website through Stripe, not in the app, and we keep a record of the purchase against your install ID.

This policy explains what the Brainpath app ("Brainpath", "we", "us") for iOS and Android collects, why, and the choices you have. Brainpath is made and sold by Sort Inc. (Convivial Concierge).

1. No account, progress on your device

You never create an account or give us your name, email, or phone number in the app. Your test results, practice progress, crowns, XP, streaks, and growth graph are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, that progress is deleted with it. We do not have a copy.

2. Anonymous usage analytics

When you first open Brainpath, the app creates a random install ID. It is not your advertising ID and is not derived from your device or from anything about you. With it, the app sends us usage events such as: app opened, session start and length, test and practice activity, ad shown, and purchase started, plus the app version, operating system version, and device language setting. We use this only to understand and improve the app and to keep it working (for example, to fix crashes and broken screens). We do not sell it and do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Brainpath shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers — on Android, the Android advertising ID; on iOS, an app-vendor identifier (Brainpath does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the iOS advertising identifier, IDFA, is not available);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device). On iOS, ads in Brainpath are not based on tracking you across other apps.

4. Purchases (Stripe, on the web)

Brainpath offers two optional purchases: Brainpath Plus, a monthly subscription, and extra IQ tests, bought one at a time (your first full test is free). The app opens our checkout web page in your browser, and payment is processed by Stripe. Your card or Apple Pay details go to Stripe and never pass through the app or our servers. Stripe collects the information needed for the payment, such as your email address, billing details, and payment method, and sends you a receipt.

To unlock what you bought, our server keeps purchase records keyed by your random install ID: the Stripe customer and payment or subscription IDs, the product, the amount and currency, the subscription status and renewal date, whether a payment was refunded, the email address Stripe gives us for the purchase, and which extra-test credits have been used. The app checks these records using its install ID. We do not receive or store your card number. You can restore purchases on a new device with the email address and receipt number from your purchase email; restoring moves these records to the new install ID.

6. Children

Brainpath is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Retention and deletion

Usage events are kept for as long as they are useful for improving the app, and no longer than 24 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for your subscription and for tax and accounting rules. To ask us to delete the analytics and purchase records tied to your install, email us (see below). Because we don't know who you are, please include your install ID or purchase receipt so we can find the right records.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal information. Email us to make a request; we will respond within the time the law requires.

9. Not a clinical test

The IQ-style score in Brainpath is a practice estimate, not a clinical IQ test, and is not medical or psychological information. It stays on your device with the rest of your progress.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
