Brainpath Support

Daily reasoning practice for iPhone and Android.

Questions, a bug, or a puzzle you think is wrong? Email gabe@convivialconcierge.com. Tell us your device, app version, and what happened (a screenshot helps). We usually reply within two business days.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up or login. Your progress is saved on your device.

I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?

Progress lives on the device, so it doesn't transfer automatically and is removed if you delete the app. Brainpath Plus and unused extra tests can be restored on a new device (see below).

Is the score a real IQ score?

No. Brainpath shows an IQ-style practice estimate, not a clinical IQ test. It is meant to help you practice and see your history, and it naturally moves up and down between tests.

What can I buy?

Your first full IQ test is free. After that you can buy an extra test for $7.00 (one test each), or subscribe to Brainpath Plus for $15.00 a month: unlimited tests, all lessons, section practice, and no ads. Purchases are made on our website through Stripe, in your browser, not in the app. After paying, tap Back to Brainpath and your purchase unlocks.

How do I restore or cancel?

To restore on a new device, tap Restore purchases in the app, then enter the email address and the receipt (or invoice) number from your purchase email. Brainpath Plus renews monthly until you cancel. To cancel, use Manage subscription in the app, the link in your Stripe receipt email, or email us and we'll do it for you; you keep Plus until the end of the paid month. Because purchases are billed by Stripe, they are not managed in App Store or Google Play subscriptions. See the terms of use for refunds.

Can I turn off ads?

Ads keep Brainpath free. Rewarded ads are always optional. On Android you can opt out of personalized ads in your device's privacy settings.

Delete my data

Deleting the app deletes your progress. To delete the anonymous usage and purchase records tied to your install, email us. See the privacy policy.
